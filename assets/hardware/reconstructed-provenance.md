# Illustrative board reconstruction

`reconstructed-board.png` is an AI-generated, photorealistic reconstruction created on October 26, 2026 with the built-in image generation tool. It is a 1672 × 941 RGBA PNG with transparent surroundings. It is a separate asset; the four official Etched photographs remain unchanged.

The reconstruction uses Etched's [official compute tray photograph](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/rwbees58/production/5465c0a1c0301168e9aa18acb49febce0dc7c358-3200x1800.png) as its edit target and Etched's [official packaged chip photograph](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/rwbees58/production/144906a8cf416b08acc145128d2bdd4900b42783-13700x10969.jpg) as a material and appearance reference. Both source photographs are available through the [Etched media kit](https://www.etched.com/media-kit).

The original tray photograph does not expose the PCB beneath its hoses and cold plates. The reconstructed PCB layout, power components, traces, connector details, and package positions are illustrative. They are not a verified Etched board, internal design, service procedure, or product specification. The asset should be labeled **Illustrative reconstruction** when visible. Its purpose is to support a continuous explanatory animation from the photographed tray to the real packaged-chip photograph.

## Registration

The generated output enlarged the chassis relative to the input canvas. It must not be overlaid using the source photograph's pixel coordinates. `reconstructed-registration.json` records the actual output dimensions, recommended crop, and approximate package centers. The crop can be mapped to a 3D tray plane without changing the PNG. There are faint stray alpha pixels outside the main chassis; the recommended crop excludes most of them.

The renderer combines the reconstructed board with a dense, source-photo-conditioned hose layer and copper material crops on shallow 3D geometry. The reconstruction should transition back to official photography for claims about physical product appearance.

## Cooling layers

It should remain labeled as an illustrative reconstruction.

`reconstructed-coldplates.png` is a 1672 × 941 RGBA PNG produced from the same official tray photograph. It isolates the visible copper surfaces. The renderer uses the clean upper-plate interior crops (x660/y198/146×130 and x867/y198/146×130) as materials on copper geometry, avoiding colored alpha fringes and any original hoses. The lower fragments are source-visible pieces, not invented additional copper banks. The generated plate cutout and its application to geometry are illustrative.

A subsequent alpha-cleanup generation changed the registration and retained some edge artifacts. It was not selected or used by the renderer; the existing asset and its interior texture crops remain unchanged.

The four official source photographs remain unchanged. Only the three files prefixed `reconstructed-` are generated imagery; neither these layers nor their 3D animation establish Etched's actual hidden board layout or maintenance procedure. `reconstructed-registration.json` records all final selected dimensions and crops.
