The included files are local WebP delivery copies produced by Etched's Sanity image service at quality 95. The rack detail and chip are limited to 3200 pixels wide, preserving their aspect ratios; the front rack and tray retain their original dimensions. The links above open the unconverted source images. The interface also provides **View original** links.

All photographs are served locally in the published site. The standalone preview embeds them, so viewing it does not request imagery from Etched's image host. No small website cutouts, enlarged card thumbnails, or older board renders are included. Searches of the current official site, its photography archive, and broader published coverage did not locate a standalone high-resolution photograph of the current bare inference board. The exposed compute tray photograph provides the available physical system detail.

## Editorial use and provenance

At the time of download, the official media kit described its photography as “for use with media publications and press.” This records the observed usage statement; it does not establish a broader licence for other uses. The photographs remain Etched's material. Their inclusion in this independent editorial study does not imply affiliation, approval, or endorsement by Etched.

Hardware mode uses these official photographs as references and surface textures in a continuous 3D scene. The rack, sliding drawer, cooling assembly, board, and chip share one coordinate system. Reversing scroll retraces the same movement; reduced motion selects stable views.

The additional `reconstructed-board.png`, `reconstructed-hoses.png`, and `reconstructed-coldplates.png` textures were generated from the official tray photograph. The hidden PCB and component arrangement are illustrative, rather than verified Etched hardware. The cooling cutouts reproduce visible material and form with approximate registration. Generation details and registration are recorded in `reconstructed-provenance.md`.

This is an explanatory reconstruction, not a documented teardown or service procedure. The chip photo is a separate official photograph; the animation selects an illustrative package position beneath one cold plate. Functional compute and HBM labels on the photographed package are marked as inferred. On-chip storage is shown only in a conceptual cutaway. Diagram layouts and animation timings do not establish Etched's actual floorplan, package internals, board connectivity, rack configuration, or measured performance.
